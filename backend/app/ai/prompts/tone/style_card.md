# Style card (what goes in `sitter_profiles.style_card`)

One short paragraph per sitter, written in the second person about how they write. It is added to every draft
prompt for that sitter, next to a few of their own past replies (`tone_samples`). Keep it to habits, never facts:

- how they greet and sign off ("Hi {name}!", "Talk soon 🐾")
- emoji: which, how many, where
- sentence length and punctuation (short and breezy, or complete and careful)
- phrases they often use, and words they avoid
- how they say "I'll confirm" / "I can't"

Never put amounts, dates, names, addresses or house rules here — the server supplies the facts, and prices and dates
are written as `{PRICE}` / `{DATE}` in stored examples and filled in from the real quote.
