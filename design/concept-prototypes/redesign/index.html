Answer “is my pet OK?” first

Owner · Home
  • One stay card replaces two “In care” cards. Photo, “With Lucy now”, day 2 of 3, and today's care progress: the README's “learn without asking” at a glance.
  • Latest update right on Home (photo + AI caption), so the owner doesn't have to open Diary to feel reassured.
  • The one primary action matches the moment: “See today's updates” during a stay. “Add pet” becomes a text link (DESIGN.md §7.1).
  • Log out leaves the header (D47: settings live in Profile). Bell + avatar only.
  • Allergy stays visible as a Heads-up tag, warning tone, not a status chip.
Components: Card Progress Bar Avatar Heads-up Tag Button (pinned via Screen footer) Icon Button

Show where the stay is

Owner · Bookings
  • Upcoming · Requests · Past segments (D47b already names Past) instead of one long list as bookings grow.
  • The 5-stage stepper on each booking (Inquiry → Home). It's the README's main story; judges see the whole flow on one card.
  • Handoffs as two clear rows with who drives and a check when done, instead of one dense paragraph. Realistic demo times (7:30 AM, not 12:37 AM).
  • Price and consents summarised on the card (03C is built; surface it).
  • Sitter row gets rating and reply speed (Rover benchmark).
Components: Segmented Control Stage Card (compact) Quote Card line Profile Card Button

Owner · Feed

The diary is the product; don't show it empty

Owner · Diary
  • “On air · Lucy” signals a live stay (D47 “Live/On air”).
  • The AI daily note leads, with the chips it was written from and “approved by Lucy”. Shows Nemotron and the no-typing sitter loop in one card (7.2 / 7.7, current focus).
  • Today's timeline below: time rail, check-ins, photos. Same data as History, organised by time.
  • Seed the demo with one sent note and a few check-ins so judges never land on “No diary yet” while Max is in care.
Components: Daily Note Suggestion Chip Notification Row-style timeline Card

One next task, one big button

Sitter · Home (D47b dashboard)
  • The next task is the hero with “Complete with photo” (DESIGN.md §7.1 example): care, snap, tap.
  • Heads-up in warning tone (live uses a green success fill with an orange border, which mixes signals).
  • Quick check-in chips one tap away instead of “tap a pet to check in” in a card.
  • Stats shrink to one progress line; the three big number tiles spend the best space on counts.
  • Treat scan (08) and the evening 5-second check as the two secondary actions. The calendar icon moves into Bookings; Log out into Profile.
  • Later (11.12): the pixel Tamagotchi status can sit above the next-task card.
Components: Task Row (Next) Heads-up Tag Filter Chip Progress Bar Button (secondary)
