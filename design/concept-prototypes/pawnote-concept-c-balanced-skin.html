    Soft tint · photo-first

    C · Balanced Skin

    Warm, modern, photo-first. The real 11.10/11.12 mechanic as a soft tint, plus a small lively status card inside an otherwise normal Home.

    UI choices

    • Plus Jakarta Sans: heavy 800 headings over regular 500 body; large radii (cards 1.25rem, photos 1rem), pill buttons. Elevation follows shadcn/ui: a hairline border plus a faint shadow on cards, a stronger shadow only for floating layers (toast, sheet).
    • Soft skin: the snapped preset tints the primary color, card surfaces, hairlines and pill states on every tab. The page background stays neutral so the photos carry the color.
    • Clean filled/duotone SVG icons; the active tab is a filled icon on a tinted pill.

    UX choices

    • Home keeps the pixel status card, then a sideways "Today's photos" strip (wheel or drag) and one "Next up" row.
    • Streak chip (11.12 stretch): "On time N days running" from past days plus today once fed. Shown from 2 days, never as "broken", and secondary to today's status.
    • Feed: one big photo card per post with Lucy, time, a mood chip and an "AI caption" label (Nemotron).
    • Care: a timed schedule where the next task gets the single primary "Complete with photo". Completing posts to Feed and confirms "Sent to owner".
    • Reports: summary first (mood + 3 stats), then highlights and Lucy's AI-drafted note.

    What to look for

    • The pet's personality lives in one status card and the photos. Is that enough presence, or does it get lost?
    • The tint reaches surfaces and pills, not the page background. Does it read as "my pet's app" or as a normal app?
    • Big photo cards make the Feed long. Does scrolling feel rewarding or slow for a 5-second check?
    • Demo shortcut: Care shows the sitter's side; the other tabs are the owner's view.
