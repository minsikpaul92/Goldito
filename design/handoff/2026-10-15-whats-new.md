## 3. Photo requests (#94): design notes

The flow works (request → sitter banner → next shared photo answers it). Small changes, all `ui-polish`:

| Now | Suggested |
| :--- | :--- |
| Waiting state is the same button, disabled and grey ("Requested · waiting for a photo") — reads as broken | A status line instead of a dead button: "Asked Chloe at 2:14 PM · her next photo will show here", with **Cancel** as a text link |
| Button label has an emoji ("📷 Request photo") | Camera icon + "Request photo" (icons, not emoji, in controls) |
| Sitter banner is a plain pressable line with an emoji ("📷 Robert asked for a photo of Max — tap to share") | `Banner` (Info): title "Robert would like a photo of Max", action **Share a photo** |
| No time on either side | Show when it was asked; after ~2 h with no photo, the owner sees "Chloe hasn't shared yet" (DESIGN.md §10: tell the owner before they ask) |
